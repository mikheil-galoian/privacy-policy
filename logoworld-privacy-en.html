Privacy Policy

Logo World app (iOS, Android). Document version 1.0-2026-09. Effective 17 September 2026.
Controller: Mikheil Galoian (individual). Contact: detailingtime-ru@detailingtime.ru.

1. In short

Logo World supports speech practice at home. Without an account it runs entirely on your device: lessons, progress and reminders never leave it. An account is only needed for the voice helper and, later, for working with a specialist. We show no ads, we do not track you, and we do not share your data with anyone for marketing.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we embed no analytics or advertising SDKs. The app creates no accounts for children: an adult registers, and the child's name may be a nickname.

3. Voice helper

When you start a session, audio is streamed to OpenAI, which recognises the speech and produces the reply. Our server authorises the session and counts the minutes used; it never receives or stores audio. OpenAI acts as our processor and, under its API terms, does not use this data to train models. See openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. Please do not read out diagnoses, documents or other details you would rather keep on the device.

4. Where data is stored

The app has two independent regions that never exchange data; you pick one when you register. The international region runs on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Supabase infrastructure); the Russian region runs on servers in the Russian Federation. Credentials and tokens on the device are kept in the system keychain (Keychain / Android Keystore) and are excluded from iCloud backups.

5. Who else sees the data

Only the processors the app cannot run without: the server and database provider, the service that delivers one-time code emails, and the voice helper provider. We do not sell data and we share nothing with ad networks or data brokers. Disclosure otherwise happens only on a lawful, justified request.

6. Your rights

You may obtain a copy of your data, correct it, withdraw consent and delete your account. Deletion is available inside the app: Cabinet → account card → “Delete account”. The account and its server-side data are removed immediately and irreversibly, and local data on the phone is erased as well. The lessons stay available without an account. For anything else write to detailingtime-ru@detailingtime.ru; we reply within 30 days.

7. Security

All traffic to our servers uses TLS. Access is constrained by row-level security in the database: an account can read only its own rows. Third-party service keys stay on the server and are never shipped inside the app.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults — parents and specialists. We deliberately avoid collecting children's data: the child's name is optional and may be fictional, and no accounts are created for children.

9. Medical notice

Logo World does not diagnose, does not prescribe treatment and does not replace an in-person consultation with a qualified specialist.

10. Changes

For material changes we publish a new version of this document and ask you to accept it at sign-in. Earlier versions are available on request.
