Logo World Support

Speech practice at home. Write to us: detailingtime-ru@detailingtime.ru — we answer on business days, usually within 24 hours.

Frequent questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lessons, the route and progress work without registration and stay on your phone. An account is only needed for the voice helper.

The code does not arrive

Check the spam folder and the address, wait a minute and request a new code. If nothing arrives, write to us with the address and the approximate time of the attempt.

How to delete the account

Cabinet → account card → “Delete account”. The account and its server data are removed immediately and irreversibly, and local data on the phone is erased. The lessons stay available without an account.

Voice helper limits

A session lasts up to 5 minutes, and up to four sessions are available per day. The counter resets at midnight UTC.

The app does not replace a specialist

Logo World does not diagnose and does not prescribe treatment. Stop the session and consult a qualified specialist if there is pain, marked discomfort or a sudden change in speech.
